# BookNest: Zero-Budget Digital Marketing Portfolio

A personal digital marketing portfolio for **BookNest, a fictional Indian online bookstore**. The project demonstrates research documentation, SEO planning, copywriting, social content production and spreadsheet measurement.

**Actual spend: ₹0. Paid advertising budget: ₹0.** No campaigns have been launched and no live performance results are claimed.

- [Portfolio report (PDF)](BookNest_Portfolio_Report.pdf): case study, strategy, creative previews, blog, email templates and measurement plan.

- [Creative kit (ZIP)](BookNest_Creative_Kit.zip): 26 upload-ready PNG slides, 26 editable SVG files, captions, alt text and research notes.

## Research and assumptions

Public homepage text from [Crossword](https://www.crossword.in/) and [SapnaOnline](https://www.sapnaonline.com/) was reviewed on **17 October 2026**. Observations concern visible discovery and merchandising features. Search functionality, usability, service quality, traffic, sales and ranking methodologies were not tested. Amazon's bookstore page could not be retrieved and remains unassessed.

The audience and positioning are hypotheses. SEO keywords are ideas: search volume, difficulty and CPC have not been measured. All dashboard performance inputs are synthetic examples, not achievements, targets or forecasts. The real-results log is intentionally blank.
